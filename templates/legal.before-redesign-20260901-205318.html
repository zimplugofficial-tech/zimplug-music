ZIMPLUG MUSIC

Legal & DMCA

Last updated: September 1, 2026

ZimPlug Music respects intellectual property rights and expects artists, creators and users to do the same.

1. Platform Role

ZimPlug Music provides a platform for music discovery and, where authorized, direct music downloads. Music and related information may be provided by ZimPlug Music, artists, creators or authorized contributors.

2. Copyright Ownership

Copyright in music, artwork, photographs, logos, recordings, compositions and other creative works belongs to their respective owners unless otherwise stated.

Uploading or making a file available through ZimPlug Music does not transfer ownership of copyright to ZimPlug Music.

3. Creator Responsibilities

  • Creators must own or have permission to distribute submitted music.
  • Creators must not knowingly upload infringing material.
  • Creators must provide accurate information about their releases.
  • Creators are responsible for obtaining required permissions from collaborators and rights holders.

4. Copyright Complaints

If you believe content on ZimPlug Music infringes your copyright, submit a detailed copyright complaint through the Report page or the official contact channel.

A useful complaint should identify the copyrighted work, identify the allegedly infringing material, provide sufficient contact information and explain the basis of the rights claim.

5. Review & Removal

ZimPlug Music may review reported content and may restrict, disable or remove content where appropriate, including where content appears to violate copyright, applicable law, platform rules or the rights of others.

6. Counter-Notices

Where applicable law provides a process for responding to a copyright removal, an affected uploader may submit information explaining why the removal was incorrect or authorized. ZimPlug Music may request additional information before taking further action.

7. Trademarks

ZimPlug Music names, logos, designs and branding may be protected by applicable intellectual property laws. They may not be used in a way that falsely suggests endorsement, partnership or ownership.

8. Prohibited Content

  • Unauthorized copyrighted music.
  • Malware or malicious files.
  • Fraudulent or deceptive material.
  • Content designed to compromise accounts or devices.
  • Content that violates applicable law.
  • Material submitted without the necessary rights or permissions.

9. Legal Requests

ZimPlug Music may respond to valid legal requests and may preserve or disclose information where required by applicable law.

10. Contact

For copyright, DMCA or other legal matters, use the official ZimPlug Music contact channel provided on the website.
