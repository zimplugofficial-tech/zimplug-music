OUR APPROACH

Respect the music.

ZimPlug Music is built around music discovery and supporting artists. We take intellectual property rights seriously and provide a way for rights holders to report material they believe infringes their rights.

01
COPYRIGHT

Content ownership

Music, artwork, photographs, names, logos and other creative material may be protected by copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property laws.

Unless otherwise stated, ZimPlug Music does not claim ownership of music or creative works uploaded by artists or other rights holders.

© 2026 ZimPlug Music ZimPlug and its branding are used as part of the ZimPlug Music platform.
02
ARTISTS & UPLOADS

Responsibility for submitted content

Anyone submitting music or other material to ZimPlug Music should have the necessary rights, permissions or authorization to distribute that material through the platform.

If you believe material has been uploaded without appropriate authorization, you can report it to us for review.

03
DMCA · TAKEDOWN

Copyright complaints

If you are a copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of one, you may contact ZimPlug Music regarding material that you believe infringes your copyright.

To help us investigate efficiently, your notice should identify the copyrighted work, identify the material you believe is infringing, and provide enough information for us to locate it.

01 Identify the work

Describe the copyrighted work or works you believe have been infringed.

02 Identify the material

Give us the song, artist, page or URL where the material appears.

03 Your contact details

Provide enough information for us to contact you about the complaint.

04 Good-faith statement

Explain why you believe the reported use is unauthorized.

04
REVIEW

What happens after a complaint?

We may review the information provided, contact the relevant parties where appropriate, and take action based on our review and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include removing or disabling access to reported material while the matter is investigated.

05
FALSE REPORTS

Please provide accurate information

Copyright complaints should only be submitted when you genuinely believe that your rights have been infringed. Please do not knowingly submit false or misleading information.

Providing inaccurate information can create serious consequences for the person making a complaint.

06
COUNTER-NOTICES

If your content was removed

If material is removed following a copyright complaint, the affected uploader may contact us regarding the decision and provide information supporting their right to use the material.

Any formal counter-notification process will be handled according to the applicable legal requirements and circumstances of the complaint.
