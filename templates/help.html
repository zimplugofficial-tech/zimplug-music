MUSIC

Music & Downloads

How do I find music?

Browse the Music section, explore genres, check featured releases, or use the search function to find songs and artists.

How do I listen to a song?

Select a song from the music listings and use the ZimPlug music player to start listening.

How do I download a song?

Open the song you want and select the Download option when it is available. Your download will then begin through your browser.

Why isn't my download working?

First, try refreshing the page and downloading the song again. If the problem continues, please report the issue so we can investigate it.

02
ACCOUNT

Your Account

Do I need an account to browse music?

No. You can browse ZimPlug Music and discover available music without creating an account.

Why should I create an account?

An account gives you access to your personal ZimPlug Music account and future member features as the platform continues to grow.

I can't log into my account

Make sure you are using the email address and password you registered with. If you still cannot access your account, contact ZimPlug support.

03
ARTISTS

For Artists

Can artists submit music?

Yes. ZimPlug Music is designed to give artists a place to share their music and connect with listeners.

What should I submit?

Submit music that you have the legal right to distribute. Make sure your artist name, song title, artwork, and other information are accurate.

Can I upload someone else's music?

You should only upload or submit music when you have the necessary rights or permission to do so.
